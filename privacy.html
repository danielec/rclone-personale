Privacy policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

This OAuth client is used exclusively by its owner, Daniele Corti, with the open-source tool rclone, to copy files between his own computer and his own Google Drive account.

  • Data accessed: only the files in the owner's Google Drive that rclone is instructed to read or write.
  • Use of data: data is used only to perform the file transfers requested by the owner.
  • Storage: the access token is stored locally in rclone's configuration file on the owner's computer. No data is sent to any server other than Google's.
  • Sharing: no data is shared with, sold to, or disclosed to any third party.
  • Other users: the client is not offered to anyone else.
  • Revoking access: access can be revoked at any time from myaccount.google.com/permissions.
